### Option 1: SGS Non-Veg - Gundappa Hotel
**Why I'm recommending this:**
Located in Cottonpet just minutes away from Majestic, SGS Non-Veg is your absolute best bet for a fast, authentic local lunch. Known for its famous Karnataka Donne Biryani crafted with aromatic green coriander rice, it directly matches your lunch timeframe. With a travel time of under 5 minutes each way (~48 minutes total including dining), you'll easily make it back to Majestic well within your 2.0-hour window.

**What to order:** Donne Biryani (Green Rice Biryani), Mutton Chops, Chicken Fry
**Price range:** Approximate cost for two: ₹400 (budget)
**Travel:** 1.7 km away, ~4 min drive each way
